RULES AND EXCEPTIONS:
- Preserve the activity: "no parking" and "no stopping" are different restrictions.
- user_classes limits the rule to the listed users. user_classes_except exempts
  the listed users from THAT rule; it never means the rule applies only to them.
- Example: parking, max_stay 2 hours, user_classes_except resident_permit means
  "Parking up to 2 hours, except resident permit holders". It does NOT mean
  "2-hour parking for residents only". Describe any separate resident rule too.
- Example: no parking, user_classes_except accessible means
  "No parking except for accessible permit holders". Never omit "except".
- Do not turn an exception to a prohibition into an unconditional permission.
- State pickup/drop-off, loading, emergency-use and other purposes when supplied.
  Preserve whether the activity is allowed or prohibited for those purposes.
- For prohibitions with fire_hydrant, bus_stop or emergency_use purposes, state
  the restriction and give the location's purpose in parentheses: "No stopping

- Include maximum stays with their units, no-return limits, and user restrictions.
- An absent maximum stay is unspecified; do not describe it as unlimited parking.
  "accessible" means accessible (HP/DV) permit holders; "resident_permit" means
  resident permit holders. Replace underscores with spaces in other labels.
- If the activity is "unusable image", say "Sign image unusable; policy needs
  review". Do not imply that parking is permitted or prohibited.

SCHEDULES:
- Include applicable days of the week, months, weeks of the month, dates, and
